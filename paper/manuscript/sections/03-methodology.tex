\section{Methodology}\label{sec:methodology}
\subsection{Dataset}
The dataset we use in this research is open source, one collected from kaggle containing 89 picture of doctor handwriting medical prescriptions from 30 different doctors in Nawabshah, Pakistan. Each medicine name is written by 3 doctors, providing various handwriting strokes and diversity with label for each image respectively. And the second dataset collected from zenodo.com that called RxHandBD: A Handwritten Prescription Word Image Dataset containing  5,578 images and 1,559 distinct text labels the dataset containing generic medicines, pharmaceutical brands, dosage forms, and clinical instructions. Later on dataset will be divided to 80/20 data split for training and tesing with the first data will containing 72 images for training and 18 images for testing. And the second dataset containing 4,463 images for training and 1,115 images for testing
